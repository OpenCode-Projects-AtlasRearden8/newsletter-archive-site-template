# Welcome to the Plain English AI Letter

Hello, friend. This letter exists for one reason: most writing about AI is
either hype or homework. This is neither. Think of it as the letter you'd
get from a friend who keeps up with this stuff and explains it plainly.

## What you can expect

- Short issues, written in plain English
- Practical steps you can actually try
- No jargon without a translation right next to it
- Education only, never medical or legal advice

> If a sentence sounds like a brochure, I rewrote it. If it still does,
> tell me and I will rewrite it again.

## Why "like a friend would"

A friend doesn't test you. A friend doesn't bury three caveats before the
point. A friend says: *here's the thing, here's why it matters, here's how
to try it this weekend.*

That is the entire editorial policy.

## Where to start

1. Read next week's issue on how chatbots actually compose text
2. Try one tool for one small real task
3. Reply and tell me what surprised you

See you in issue two.
