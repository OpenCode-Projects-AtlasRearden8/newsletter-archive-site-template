Why run AI locally at all?

Because the privacy story is simple: nothing leaves your house. No account, no cloud, no transmission. A small model on your own computer is enough for drafting, summarizing, and Q&A.

What "local AI" means

You download a model file (once) and run it with a program called an inference runner. Popular free options include:

  • Ollama — simplest start, runs from one command
  • LM Studio — friendly graphical app (one-time download)
  • llama.cpp — for people who like buttons with fewer of them
Suggested first prompt: "Explain what a spreadsheet is, in three short sentences, to someone who has never used one." Then change "three" to "seven" and watch how answers shift.

Pick a small model first

Start with a 1–3 billion parameter model over a slow Wi-Fi morning coffee. It's really about patience management, not benchmark scores.

Your five-minute start

  1. Install Ollama
  2. Run one small model
  3. Ask it about something you know deeply — judge the answer by what you know, not by confidence of tone
